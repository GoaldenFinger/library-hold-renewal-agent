System Policy & Safety Protections

- Overdue Fine Calculation: Synthetic simulation fines are assessed at ₹5/day in INR calculated relative to UTC dates.

- Hold Protection: Renewal is blocked if another patron is waiting in the hold queue.

- Human Approval Boundary: Spoken or conversational text intents cannot execute state changes. Approval buttons remain the only approval mechanism.
